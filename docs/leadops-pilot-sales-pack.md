# LeadOps First Paid Pilot — Sales & Evidence Pack

## Executive rule
Until we have real prospect or pilot evidence, new work must serve **Sell, Deliver, or Learn**. Broad product expansion is deferred.

## What we sell
Do not lead with AI, agents, orchestration, or model names.

Lead with the operational outcome:
- fewer valuable inbound leads lost or ignored;
- faster, more consistent qualification;
- clearer lead priority;
- human review for important or ambiguous cases;
- simple follow-up tracking without forcing a full CRM project.

## First customer profile
Primary ICP for validation:
- small service businesses, agencies, consultancies, or owner-led B2B firms;
- meaningful inbound lead flow already exists;
- each qualified lead has enough value that missed or slow follow-up hurts;
- a founder, owner, sales lead, or small team can make a pilot decision quickly;
- no enterprise procurement or complex integration required for the first pilot.

Avoid as first customers:
- organizations needing a full CRM replacement;
- buyers demanding deep integrations before validation;
- low-value lead businesses where qualification has little economic impact;
- regulated healthcare workflows during this first commercial proof phase.

## 14-day Founder-led Pilot
Scope:
- one inbound workflow;
- one operator;
- limited lead volume;
- founder-led setup and onboarding;
- deterministic qualification plus AI-assisted response;
- human handoff where needed;
- simple operator view and follow-up state;
- direct support during the pilot.

The pilot is paid. The approved validation band is **SAR 2,500–5,000 one-time**, depending on lead volume and workflow complexity. The final quote is confirmed after the fit check; record buyer reaction and do not discount or expand scope without evidence.

## Prospect qualification gate
A prospect is a strong pilot candidate when most of these are true:
1. They receive inbound leads consistently.
2. Speed or consistency of qualification is a real pain.
3. A missed qualified lead has meaningful economic value.
4. A decision maker can participate directly.
5. One workflow can be isolated for a 14-day test.
6. Success can be measured without a large integration project.

## 3–5 minute demo narrative
1. **Problem:** valuable inbound requests arrive, but teams often respond inconsistently or too slowly.
2. **Capture:** show the LeadOps intake form.
3. **Qualification:** explain that score and priority use explicit rules, not AI opinion alone.
4. **AI assistance:** show contextual response generation with safe fallback.
5. **Human handoff:** show that ambiguous or important leads are escalated instead of silently automated.
6. **Operator follow-up:** show status, priority, contact/follow-up progression, and won/lost outcome.
7. **Pilot close:** propose one narrow 14-day workflow and agree on the metrics before starting.

We are running a focused 14-day founder-led pilot around one inbound workflow so we can measure whether it reduces missed opportunities and improves follow-up before expanding anything.

## Pilot evidence scorecard
Capture a baseline before the pilot and compare at the end.

Minimum evidence fields:
- inbound leads received;
- leads classified qualified / unqualified / needs-human;
- median or typical first-response time if the customer can provide it;
- number of leads requiring human review;
- follow-up progression: new → contacted → won/lost where observable;
- qualitative operator feedback: time saved, clarity gained, friction introduced;
- any confirmed missed-lead or slow-response examples before the pilot;
- willingness to continue, expand, or pay more after the pilot.

Do not invent ROI, conversion lift, time savings, or customer claims. Only publish results supported by actual pilot evidence and customer permission.

## Pricing validation
Treat pricing as a hypothesis, not a fact.
During early conversations record:
- quoted pilot price;
- buyer reaction;
- objections;
- whether value or budget is the real blocker;
- whether setup, support, or ongoing usage is perceived as most valuable;
- willingness to continue after 14 days.

Do not widen paid acquisition or commit to a public pricing model until a small number of qualified conversations provide evidence.

## Acquisition order
1. Founder-led direct outreach to a tightly defined prospect cohort.
2. Short live/demo-driven conversations.
3. First paid pilot.
4. Evidence and testimonial/case study only if permission is granted.
5. Then test controlled paid acquisition such as Meta Ads.

Paid ads are a validation amplifier after the offer and funnel are proven, not a substitute for customer conversations.

## Expansion gate
No major new product build until one of these is true:
- a real prospect repeatedly requests the same missing capability and it blocks a sale;
- a live pilot requires it for delivery;
- measured pilot evidence justifies expansion.

Otherwise defer it.
