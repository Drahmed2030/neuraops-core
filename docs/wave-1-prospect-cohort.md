## Purpose

## Qualification rule
A prospect stays in Wave 1 only when we can identify: (1) a decision-maker or founder-level buyer, (2) a visible inbound enquiry path, and (3) a plausible reason lead prioritization could matter. Pain is not assumed; it must be validated in conversation.

## Pre-send checklist
Before any message is sent:
- re-open the company's current site and confirm the route still works;
- verify buyer role is current;
- personalize one specific observation from the company's current inbound path;
- do not claim to know they have a lead-management problem;
- ask for a fit check, not a large commitment;
- use one contact route per prospect in the first touch;
- log date, channel, copy variant, response and objection;
- no paid ad spend until direct-message resonance is measured.
