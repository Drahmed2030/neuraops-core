## Touch 2 — 30–45 second product video
Send only after no reply or positive curiosity from Touch 1.

**Script / sequence:**
1. On-screen text: “Not every inbound lead deserves the same response time.”
2. Show a sample enquiry arriving.
3. Show need / budget / urgency / authority captured.
4. Show explainable score and status.
5. Show `needs human` handoff for an ambiguous case.
6. Show operator follow-up state.
7. End card: “One inbound workflow. 14 days. Measure the result.”
8. CTA: “Open to a quick fit check?”

Do not claim customer results, ROI, saved hours, conversion lift, or logos.
