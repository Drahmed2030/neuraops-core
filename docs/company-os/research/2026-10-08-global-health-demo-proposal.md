## Recommended product story

**NeuraOps — From changed result to verified closure.**

A 3–4 minute synthetic cardiology workflow spanning an existing-source simulator,
Health Cloud institutional authorization and a Cliniverse WORK review surface.
The user's cardiology expertise supports a credible scenario; medical accuracy
of synthetic case content still needs review. No autonomous diagnosis or treatment.

1. A synthetic Echo report is revised (v1 → v2), preserving its source identity.
2. Health Cloud creates/updates assigned review work with organisation, facility,
   department, principal and entitlement boundaries.
3. An entitled clinician sees the source-linked change in WORK; LEARN stays an
   independent educational product and cannot expose institutional tasks.
4. A stale acknowledgement is rejected; refresh and acknowledge the actual
   version. Replaying the same operation returns the original receipt.
5. Show the closure receipt: who, what version, scope, when, review outcome and
   audit/provenance links. Acknowledgement is not a claim of clinical resolution.
6. Demonstrate wrong-department denial and model-unavailable operation: core work
   remains deterministic. Optional AI may explain a diff with sources, never
   provide the permission or certify the clinical decision.

This is a proposed demonstrable advantage, not a claim of unique market novelty.
Dedalus already markets interoperability/coordination; DrDoctor markets a system
of action and patient communications. Broad integration or AI claims alone will
not differentiate NeuraOps. The narrow opportunity is a visibly accountable,
portable, version-bound institutional review journey with a clinician-facing app.

## Selective standards/open-source use

- Synthea: possible synthetic FHIR fixture source; use only needed records after
  license/provenance and fixture-quality review. No bulk dataset ingestion needed.
- HL7 FHIR: map a selected supported version's report/observation identifiers and
  version changes to the existing internal contract. A custom adapter is not a
  claim of complete FHIR conformance. Subscriptions are optional; a deterministic
  simulated event can demonstrate the same business boundary without new infra.
- DICOMweb: preserve the future imaging boundary. Do not add a new viewer to the
  conference's critical path; reuse already verified licensed Echo/ECG assets if
  appropriate, without claiming them to be a live hospital PACS connection.
- No new orchestration framework, identity vendor, vector database or model
  fine-tuning before the event without a demonstrated requirement.

## Participation and commercial objective

Default: low-cost attendee route plus targeted meetings. Startup Pod is optional
after written eligibility, total cost and delivery deadlines are established.
Aim for 8–12 relevant conversations and 2–3 design-partner follow-ups; these are
targets, not promised deals. Prioritize hospital digital transformation, clinical
operations/quality, integration partners, and medical education leads separately.

Prepare a three-minute demo, a 90-second clearly labelled backup video, one-page
solution brief, six-slide pitch, QR to an honest demonstration/meeting page, and
a nonbinding pilot scope. Record only consented business contact details.
Do not call the company incorporated, clinically approved, production-ready or
connected to a hospital unless the corresponding evidence exists.
