# NeuraOps LeadOps — Customer Acquisition System v1

## Executive objective
Move from product readiness to the first paid pilot using a disciplined multi-channel acquisition system. Optimize for qualified buyer conversations, not vanity reach.

## Core commercial rule
We do not sell AI. We sell a better inbound-lead operating outcome:
- fewer valuable leads missed;
- faster qualification;
- clearer priority;
- human review for important or ambiguous cases;
- simpler follow-up without forcing a full CRM project.

## Primary ICP
Prioritize owner-led or small-team service businesses where one qualified lead is economically meaningful and the buying cycle is short.

Initial verticals:
1. Digital marketing / performance agencies
2. Web / software development agencies
3. B2B consultancies
4. Recruitment / staffing firms
5. High-value home / professional services with inbound enquiries

Strong-fit signals:
- clear website lead form or booking CTA;
- evidence of active demand generation;
- multiple services or lead types requiring prioritization;
- founder/owner/sales lead publicly identifiable;
- no enterprise procurement requirement;
- one inbound workflow can be isolated for 14 days.

## Channel order
### 1. Founder-led direct outreach — primary
Use a small, high-quality prospect cohort first. Personalized email, LinkedIn DM, or verified contact route. Do not mass blast.

### 2. LinkedIn — authority + direct conversations
Use founder posts, short demo clips, problem-led posts, and personalized outreach to founders, owners, sales leaders, and agency principals.

### 3. Meta — controlled paid validation
Use Facebook + Instagram only after direct-message copy and offer language show signs of resonance. Paid ads amplify a tested message; they do not replace buyer conversations.

### 4. Partner channel
Target marketing agencies, web agencies, CRM consultants, and automation freelancers who can introduce LeadOps to multiple end clients.

### 5. Communities
Join relevant founder, agency, sales-ops, automation, and SMB communities. Contribute useful workflow examples; do not drop links without context.

## First outreach cohort
Start with 20 prospects total:
- 8 agencies
- 4 consultancies
- 4 recruitment/staffing firms
- 4 high-value service businesses

Quality gate: no prospect enters Wave 1 unless we can identify the buyer role, a real inbound-lead path, and one concrete reason LeadOps could matter.

## Outreach sequence
### Touch 1 — personalized relevance
One observation, one pain hypothesis, one 14-day pilot ask. No long product description.

### Touch 2 — short demo
Send a 30–60 second screen/demo clip showing inbound lead → score → priority → human handoff.

### Touch 3 — proof-oriented follow-up
Offer a narrow fit check around one workflow and define what would be measured during 14 days.

Stop after a bounded sequence. Do not spam.

## English first-contact pattern
Subject / opener: A quick idea for your inbound leads

Hi [Name] — I noticed [specific inbound path / service context]. If your team is manually deciding which inbound enquiries deserve attention first, we built a narrow LeadOps workflow that scores and prioritizes them, then sends unclear or important cases to a human. We are validating it through a founder-led 14-day paid pilot on one workflow only. Worth a quick fit check?

## Arabic first-contact pattern
مرحبًا [الاسم] — لاحظت [ملاحظة محددة عن مسار استقبال العملاء/الخدمة]. إذا كان فريقكم يحدد يدويًا أي الطلبات الواردة تستحق الأولوية، بنينا LeadOps كسير عمل ضيق يقوم بالتأهيل وترتيب الأولوية ثم يحوّل الحالات المهمة أو غير الواضحة لشخص حقيقي. نختبره حاليًا عبر Pilot مدفوع لمدة 14 يومًا على سير عمل واحد فقط. هل يناسبكم فحص ملاءمة سريع؟

## Creative system
### Video A — problem hook (15–20 sec)
Purpose: stop the scroll.
Opening: "Not every inbound lead deserves the same response time."
Visual: incoming requests pile up → one high-value lead is visually missed → LeadOps sorts and highlights priority.
CTA: "See how LeadOps qualifies inbound leads."

### Video B — product proof (30–45 sec)
Purpose: show the workflow, not abstract AI.
Sequence:
1. Lead arrives
2. Need / budget / urgency / authority captured
3. Explainable score appears
4. Qualified / unqualified / needs-human decision
5. Human handoff when needed
6. Operator follow-up view
7. 14-day pilot CTA

### Video C — founder explanation (30–60 sec)
Purpose: trust.
Script frame:
"We built NeuraOps LeadOps for small service teams that already receive valuable inbound leads but still qualify them manually. It does not replace your CRM or sales team. It helps decide who needs attention first, and it escalates uncertain cases to a human. We are validating one workflow at a time in a 14-day founder-led pilot."

## Video production requirements
- vertical 9:16 master for Reels / Stories;
- captions burned in or platform captions checked;
- message understandable with sound off;
- first value signal within 2–3 seconds;
- use real product UI or clearly labelled illustrative scenes;
- no fake customer logos, performance metrics, or testimonials;
- Arabic and English versions;
- end card: NeuraOps LeadOps + 14-day Pilot + clear CTA.

## Static creative set
1. "Stop treating every inbound lead the same" — score/priority visual
2. "Qualified / Needs human / Not ready" — three-state workflow
3. "One inbound workflow. 14 days. Measure the result." — pilot card

## Meta test design
Do not launch until the direct-outreach message and creative are reviewed.
Initial paid test must be small and reversible.
Primary objective: qualified pilot conversations, not impressions or likes.
Creative test:
- Video A vs Video B
- one static control
- Arabic and English separated where practical

Do not scale budget until we see qualified conversations or a paid pilot.

## Measurement dashboard
Track weekly:
- prospects researched
- verified decision makers
- first contacts sent
- reply rate
- positive reply rate
- fit-check calls
- pilots proposed
- pilots paid
- main objections
- quoted pilot price and reaction
- channel source

## Success gate
The system is working when we can repeatedly turn a small qualified prospect cohort into buyer conversations and at least one paid pilot. Until then, optimize message, ICP, and offer before adding product scope.
